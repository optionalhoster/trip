The route

All times on this page are local. Michigan through New York City is on Eastern time (ET). Hammond is on Central time (CT), 1 hour behind.

    What changed: The route no longer goes through Canada. Day 2 now ends in Erie, PA, and you reach Niagara on Day 3 morning. New York City is added for Saturday night. New Hampshire is just the Kancamagus Highway now. Meals are HMS-certified halal restaurants or food you pack.
    Book these now

    Hotels: 5 nights

    Each hotel is 1 room with 2 queen beds for 4 adults. Oct 10–11 is the Columbus Day holiday weekend, so book nights 4 and 5 first. Look for free cancellation, free parking, and a fridge and microwave. When you book, tap Edit plan and add the hotel name and confirmation number so everyone has it.

    Most hotels check in from 3–4 PM and check out by 11 AM. Every arrival is after check-in opens and every departure is before checkout, so you won't pay early or late fees. No hotel on Sun Oct 11.

        On the road

        Tips and backup plans

        If you fall behind

        • Day 2: skip Fort Mackinac and take an earlier ferry back.
        • Day 3: skip Terrapin Point and drive straight to lunch.
        • Day 5: skip Kennebunkport (saves about 40 min).
        • Day 6: skip sunrise and leave at 6:30 AM.

        Weather and packing

        Mornings on Whiteface and in the White Mountains can be near freezing. The Maine coast is windy. Pack a warm layer, a rain jacket and good walking shoes.

        No signal zones

        Northern Michigan, the Adirondacks and the Kancamagus have spotty or no cell service. Download offline maps and fill the tank before these stretches.

        Prayer on the road

        Bring a compass or a qibla app with offline mode, plus a travel prayer mat. Rest areas and state parks have quiet spots.
