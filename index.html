        On the road

        Tips and backup plans

        If you fall behind

        • Day 2: skip Fort Mackinac and take an earlier ferry back.
        • Day 3: skip Terrapin Point and drive straight to lunch.
        • Day 5: skip Kennebunkport (saves about 40 min).
        • Day 6: skip sunrise and leave at 6:30 AM.

        Weather and packing

        Mornings on Whiteface and in the White Mountains can be near freezing. The Maine coast is windy. Pack a warm layer, a rain jacket and good walking shoes.

        No signal zones

        Northern Michigan, the Adirondacks and the Kancamagus have spotty or no cell service. Download offline maps and fill the tank before these stretches.

        Prayer on the road

        Bring a compass or a qibla app with offline mode, plus a travel prayer mat. Rest areas and state parks have quiet spots.
